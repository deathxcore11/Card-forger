# Riding controls v2 (Randall, 27 Nov 2026, 6:02 PM ET)

Four face buttons each do one job. The stick (or WASD) chooses which trick. That gives 20+ moves without input strings.

## The scheme

| Job | Pad | Keyboard / mouse | Touch | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steer / trick direction | Left stick | WASD | Left thumb | Carves on the ground; picks the trick in the air |
| Ollie | A (bottom) | Space | Jump button | Hold to crouch and build pop; release to launch higher |
| Flip | X (left) | Left click or J | Flip button | Fast, snappy flips; safe |
| Grab | B (right) | Right click or K | Grab button | Hold for stylish grabs; big Flow, riskier landing |
| Grind / wall ride | Y (top) | F or L | Grind button | Snaps to beams, ruin edges, stone cards and walls |
| Spin | LB / RB | Q / E | Swipe | 180, 360, 720 in the air; multiplies Flow |
| Manual / revert | RT (hold on landing) | Ctrl (hold on landing) | Manual button | Keeps the combo alive on flat ground |

On foot, Shift stays dash, and C stays ride or slide. Mouse look stays as it is. The old J/K/L keys still work as aliases.

## Move list (direction + button)

- **Flips (tap flip in the air):** Left Kickflip, Right Heelflip, Up Impossible, Down Pop Shuvit. Tap twice for a Double Flip, with extra Flow if there is time in the air.
- **Grabs (hold grab in the air):** Left Melon, Right Indy (tighter hitbox), Up Nosegrab, Down Method (huge Flow, slow recovery).
- **Grinds (grind near an edge):** Neutral 50-50, Left or Right Boardslide (sparks), Up Nosegrind (extra speed), Down 5-0 (wide elemental trail).
- **Wall ride:** grind near a mesa or ruin wall to ride along it. Ollie off the wall is a Wallplant, which kicks off for big height and speed.
- **Linking:**
  - Hold manual on landing for a Manual.
  - Tap manual at the bottom of a ramp for a Revert that chains into a Manual.
  - Grab on the ground is a Powerslide, a sharp drift that kills speed to dodge a red line.
  - Down + grab in the air is a Slam Drop: dive straight down, and the summons' slam shockwave fires on impact.
- **Specials:** come from a Special meter that fills from Flow, not from a multiplier value. When it is full the card glows gold, and Up + Down + flip or grab (or the one Special button on touch) fires a signature move: Deck Shuffle, Tableflip or Seal Grind.

## Three horde rules

1. **No balance bar.** Manuals and grinds get a 2.5 s combo timer instead. It refills on every new rail, ramp or landed trick. Eyes stay on the horde.
2. **Manual on a held button**, never Up-Down on the stick, so steering stays smooth.
3. **Tricks keep you alive.** An ollie pop and a Slam Drop landing give 0.25 s of i-frames, and a Slam Drop shockwave erases nearby enemy shots.

## Moving around the world

Getting around the big map, on foot and on the card, must feel great, not only the tricks. Riding carries speed over dunes. Long lines chain kickers, rails and quarters across a cell. Getting on and off the card is instant. Walking is never a chore between areas.
