# Reference clip notes, 23 Nov 2026 (four clips, 1:44 to 5:27; frames sampled every 10 to 30 s, so motion is not seen)

Feel only, in our own words. Our colour rules win over anything in the clips (no gold banners unless it is a "use me" prompt; hearts and warnings follow our palette).

- **Level-up beat.** The whole picture takes a burst of straight light rays from the centre, a thin multicolour bar runs along the top edge, and a panel offers three stat or weapon cards side by side with three small buttons under them (remove, skip, redraw). The world stays visible behind the rays. The rays are the loudest part and they are drawn behind the panel, never over the cards.
- **Chest open.** A narrow panel in the middle with a bright vertical beam rising out of the chest and a few sparks; the item appears at the top of the beam.
- **Hordes.** Fights at the 2 to 3 minute mark show well over a hundred small enemies in one frame, thick as a crowd, each one a flat green creature; the player stays readable because of a bright outline and a red marker under the feet.
- **Damage numbers.** Yellow numbers pop above hits and stack; a bigger "crit" word with the number on strong hits; numbers rise and fade in under a second.
- **Weapon arcs.** A sword shows as a white crescent that sweeps round the duelist, the same size as its true hit; the big boss fights show several crescents at once.
- **Boss.** A wide red health bar across the top with the name, a second boss added mid-fight, and the screen going almost white for about a second when a boss falls.
- **Shrines and the exit.** A shrine offers a short list of rewards or a "spurn" option; the exit is a stone ring with a white swirl on the ground round it and a task line "Enter the portal".
- **Stage change.** The next stage opens with a full colour shift (cyan sky and green grass to purple and blue) and a title card in the middle for a few seconds.
- **HUD.** Top left: health bar, a small grid of owned weapon and item icons, and a task list. Top right: a round minimap with a compass. Stat panel on the right during offers.

What this changes for us: the level-up beat and the boss-fall white-out are the two big moments worth matching in our own style; hordes of 100+ readable creatures are the target the stand-in creatures and the Tier 5 look must hold.


## Spectacle queue: two designs from the clip notes (queued, NOT built; our own looks)

1. Level-up beat. When the offer panel opens, card-fan rays stand behind it: five to seven pale rays fanning out from the panel's top edge, each a flat card-shaped sliver (5:7 ratio, ink edge, one tone from the leading element's ramp), slowly turning out by about 12 degrees over the half second the panel takes to arrive, then still. The world behind dims by the existing dim term, nothing moves in the sim. Class B moment (no hit-stop). Flash: none. Comfort switch: rays appear without the turn.
2. Boss-fall white-out. One second, class A. The screen is flooded with a pale flash that is stepped through the ramp, never smooth: three hard steps (bright, then body, then shade) at 0.10 s, 0.35 s and 0.70 s, then clear, with the boss's fall pose held still behind it. Strength is the FlashGate's stage-capped value (never pure white; the ramp's bright tone of the boss's element). The comfort switch (Reduce flashes) replaces it with a 1.0 s dim and a slow fade, no steps. Hit-stop 80 ms, from sim ticks only, as always.

Both go through the director (one moment owns the screen at a time) and neither touches the sim or the replay hash.
