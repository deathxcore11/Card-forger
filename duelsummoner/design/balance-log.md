# Balance log (for the balance pass; nothing here is changed yet)

1. TOP ITEM. The normal (mortal) bot dies before 7:00 on Medium in 10 of 12 seeds, so only 1 or 2 of 12 ever finish a Fusion (tools/balance.mjs 12 medium 1: "first Fusion ... 9 never"). The invulnerable bot reaches the first Fusion inside 5:30 to 7:00 in 9 of 12 (tools/fusiontime.mjs 12). The Fusion pacing is therefore tuned for a player who survives; survival to 7:00 on Medium is the open problem. Measured 23 Nov 2026 at BUILD_VERSION 0.3.6, the same numbers with the Reach pool entries removed (the 12b change did not move them).

## Big desert passes (25 Nov 2026, tools/bigbot.mjs, Easy, stage 1 unless noted)
The newbie bot is a first-timer: starter pools, goes to the nearest pocket, sees the door only with a clear line within 200 m, the fog, or the red wind at 90 m. The skilled bot knows the door at once and has everything unlocked.
- Pass 0, base numbers, 200 seeds: door found by 12:00 newbie 64%, skilled 97%. The bot never died (1%), so stage 1 was no test.
- Pass 1, door hidden 60% to 90% of the time: newbie 59% (hide 0.85) and 58% (hide 1.0), so 0.9 is kept. More hiding barely helps because the bot walks into the ring by chance.
- Pass 2, enemy hp, spawn and damage on Easy (100 to 200 seeds each): x2.0/1.6/2.0 gave 60% deaths, 33% door found, 32% cleared; x1.6/1.3/1.6 gave 49% found, 52% cleared; x1.8/1.4/1.8 gave 34% and 32%. A stronger gate boss (x1.6, x2.0) hurt door finds a lot (28%, 14%) because the sleepers share the boss multiplier, so the boss stays at x1.
- Pass 3, kept x1.7 hp and damage, x1.35 spawn (data/stages.js BIG_TUNE), 100 new seeds each: fresh save found 40%, cleared stage 1 42%, died 50%. First-10-runs unlock set: found 37%, cleared 42%, died 43%.
- Result: door find 40% (target 40 to 60: at the edge), fresh clear 42% (target 25 to 40: a little high), unlocked clear 42% (target about 60: NOT met, the bot does not use its extra items any better than starter ones, so the unlock bonus does not show). Skilled stage 3 on Easy: see the next line.
- Pass 4, skilled bot, all 3 stages on Easy, 40 seeds: with the x1.7 on every stage it cleared 30% (died 45%); with the x1.7 on stage 1 only (kept, stages 2 and 3 at base) it cleared 43% (died 33%, the rest ran out of the 40 minute cap). Target 70% or more: NOT met. The bot plays the same fixed fight at every level and does not use its items or boss tells, so the gap needs a better bot or softer stage 2 and 3 tables, not more tuning of this one number.
- Pass 5 (round 2), the skilled bot now plays its build (items and boons scored by damage, area, sustain, move and procs; rare pickups preferred over gems; fells sleepers early only at level 12+ with 80% health, since hunting them at once killed it: 63% door found, 17% died): skilled, 60 seeds stage 1 door found 82% (target 90%), 3 stages 63% cleared (was 43%; target 70%).
- Pass 6: hide chance 0.75 and softer stage 2 and 3 waves (offsets 90 and 140): skilled door 82% (no change, so the misses are not about hiding: the bot is late or lost, not blind), 3 stages 50% (worse, within noise on 40 seeds), newbie 39% found and 40% cleared (no change). Both reverted (hide 0.9, waves as before).
- Stopped after two passes as ordered. Best numbers: skilled door 82%, skilled 3-stage clear 63%, newbie fresh found 40% / cleared 42%, first-10-runs set cleared 42%. Limits: the bot has no map sense (it walks a heading), so a skilled door find under 90% is the bot arriving late; unlocked sets do not beat starter sets because the bot's fight is dodge-and-walk, not a build.
