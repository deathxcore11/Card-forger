# Reference run breakdown, 23 Nov

**Source.** The owner made four recordings of one run of a commercial 3D horde-survival game (files `0045`, `0051`, `0054`, `0057`, about 12 min of video). They cover **one full stage**: from 0:40 elapsed to the stage timer reaching 0:00, then 33 s of overtime, the portal, and the arrival plus the first ~7 s of stage 2. The game was set to **Easy, Tier 3**.

**How to read this doc.**
- All reference numbers are read off the HUD. Anything marked GUESS is my inference.
- "Elapsed" means 10:00 minus the stage countdown. Both clocks freeze in every menu.
- The reference is described by structure and numbers. Its on-screen words appear in quotes only as evidence of what was seen; none of them may be reused in Duel Summoner. Every name on the "ours" side is Duel Summoner's.
- `design/environment-bar.md` does not exist in the repo. The world-scale bar used here is `generator-spec.md` §18.

- **Gold** is roughly 1.6 per kill overall (1.1-2.0 by minute from minute 3 on; minute 2 is ~3.3). Income ramps from ~130/min (minute 2, the first fully observed minute) to ~580/min.

4. GUESS: if the boss is not dead at 0:00, overtime simply runs until it is.

## 3. Systems observed

**Two clocks.**
- The stage countdown sits big at the top centre. An elapsed clock sits small at the top left.
- Both freeze in every menu. Their sum drifted only ~3 s over the stage.

**Objective checklist.**
- Three lines under the slots: Find the Boss Portal → Defeat the Boss → Enter the Portal. A new line appears only when the previous one ticks.
- A done line turns green with a check. The new stage starts with line 1 only, sliding in ~5 s after arrival.

**Charge shrines.**
- A white pillar with a floating "!". Standing near it raises a translucent dome, about 5-10 m in radius (GUESS). The dome colour varied (cyan, purple, yellow-green, blue).
- Charging takes **~5 s**. Enemies keep coming and converge on the dome edge.
- Reward: pick 1 of 3 stat boons, or "Ignore Offers". The game pauses.
- Each boon rolls its own rarity:

| Rarity | Card colour | Example values |
|---|---|---|
| Common | green | +5% luck, +15 max HP, +12% damage, +8% move speed, +5% crit, +10% knockback, +5% armor, +20 HP regen, +6% lifesteal |
| Uncommon | teal | +18 max HP, +9.6% duration |
| Rare | purple | +14% projectile speed, +7% evasion |
| Epic | dark red | +12.8% size |

- Values scale by rarity: max HP goes +15 (Common) → +18 (Uncommon), so each rarity step is roughly ×1.2 (GUESS).
- About 9 shrines were used in the stage (3-4 before 0:41). They cluster 10-20 m apart early, and one sits beside the gate arena.
- The pause screen keeps a **"Shrine Logs"** list of every boon taken.
- A risk totem gave +5% Difficulty, logged the same way.

**Upgrade offers (level-up).**
- 3 cards. A card is either NEW (with a one-line description) or an upgrade to something owned ("LVL n", with **every changed stat as old → new, the new value in green**).
- A weapon upgrade rolls 1-2 random stat lines. A tome upgrade is 1 stat.
- **Rarity sets the step size**:

| Upgrade | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Epic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crit tome | +7 pts | +8 | +10 | |
| Attack-speed tome | +8% | +9% | | |
| Weapon projectile count | +1 | +1.2 | +1.4 | +1.6, plus size +32% vs +20% (Common) |

- Limits: 4 weapon slots and 4 tome slots. Items are unlimited (10 by the end). All slots were full by L5 (weapons) and L7 (tomes), so from then on every offer is an upgrade.
- Most level-ups also grant a **free random micro-stat** (GUESS: not every level showed one), shown as a white popup right of the hero: +8.9% projectile speed, +9.1 max HP, +3.3% attack speed, and so on.
- Max HP +1 per level.

**Banish / Skip / Refresh.**
- **7 / 7 / 7** charges, each count shown above its button.
- Refresh: the first use was free (GUESS), then it cost 5 gold, then 25.
- Chests show a Banish count of 7 beside Take (GUESS: the same pool; never used, so unconfirmed).
- The player used Refresh twice in 32 levels and Banish/Skip never.

**Chests.**
- Placed on the ground with a floating price and an E prompt. The HUD always shows the next price.
- **Price climbs by a near-constant step, not a multiplier: 30 → 72 → 115 → 159 → 206** (+42, +43, +44, +47). The price carried into stage 2 unchanged.
- Free chests (GUESS for all sources): they opened right after each boss died, at some ground spots, and mid-fight at 3:48. A free-open roll from key items can't explain the ones at 1:11 and 3:48, because the key was bought at 6:06.
- Rarity stays hidden until the reveal (~2-4 s):
  1. the chest scales in, with an Open button;
  2. a beam rises in the rarity colour, **shifting cyan → pink for Epic**;
  3. item icons sometimes cycle like a slot machine;
  4. a white starburst, then the rarity word, name and one line of effect;
  5. Banish or Take.

**Merchant.**
- 3 items with prices of 22-57 gold. One is a trade-off (+20% damage, −5% max HP) and one is a curse (+7% difficulty).
- The merchant gives a one-line taunt as you leave.

**Map power-ups.**
- Screen-clear: about +190 kills from the blast itself, and +247 kills and +300 gold across that 10 s window.
- Speed burst.
- Gold frenzy: a golden vignette and a big $ counter at the top for 20 s+.
- A stopwatch was also seen (GUESS: freezes time).
- Each shows a white popup with a joke tag.

**Swarm warnings.**
- A yellow "SWARM INCOMING" banner for 5-6 s at 4:00 and 7:01. After swarm 2, the on-screen count goes from ~10 to 100-150 within ~15-20 s. Swarm 1 peaked at about 40+.
- A pink-red "FINAL SWARM" banner at 0:00.
- The two mid-stage surges produced 2 and 4 level-ups within 30 s. The final swarm produced 1.

**Bosses and telegraphs.**
- Timed boss 1: 1,067 HP, 4-5x hero height, killed in 54 s.
- Timed boss 2: 4,382 HP, killed in 48 s.
- Gate boss: 16.2K HP, 6-8x hero height, 3:10 from summon to death, but mostly ignored until 7:48. Its bar dropped about 800 HP while it was off screen (GUESS: thorns and splash damage).
- Every attack has a **red translucent ground disc or dome, 1-3 hero widths across, 0.5-1.5 s before impact**, then spikes or rocks burst out. Patterns: a single drop on the hero, trails of 5-7 rings along the path, and rings of 6-8 with two bosses up.
- One boss hit was **17 HP (12.7% of max)**.
- Boss bars sit under the clock with "cur / max" (K-abbreviated). Two bosses split the bar into two halves. When one dies, the other widens.

**Elites.**
- A floating yellow crown, 1.5-4x hero height, 2-5 on screen at once.
- Elite Spawn and Damage to Elites are player stats.
- One elite kill showed a $90 popup (GUESS: elite drop).
- The worst hit of the run was **65 HP in 1.5 s from an elite's contact damage with no telegraph.**

**Difficulty label.**
- The pause card shows the biome, "Tier 3" and a meta-currency multiplier of ×1.2.
- The difficulty name ("Easy") and the climbing ×N badge show **only in overtime**.
- Difficulty is also a player stat (5%).

**Map and minimap.**
- Full map from the pause menu: square, heavy fog of war, about 25% revealed at 0:48 and about 60% at 5:30. It showed about 25 chest icons and 15 shrine-type icons.
- The minimap is round and **rotates with the camera** (compass letters move on the rim). Icons: chests yellow and shrines cyan (both GUESS), boss red skull, portal cyan swirl.

**Low-HP state.**
- A red screen-edge vignette, on at 26% HP (27/105) and off at 29% (30/105), so the threshold is about 27-28%.

**Meta currency.**
- 1,381 → 1,402 over 10.5 min (+21), even with ×1.9 gain. A slow trickle.

## 4. Readability: why a 100-150-enemy screen stays readable, and where it breaks

**What works**
1. **One danger colour.** Every enemy attack is red-orange, semi-transparent, on the ground, and appears 0.5-1.5 s early. The grass is green, the sky cyan, enemies green, grey or white, so red owns "move now". The hero took one 17 HP hit in the minute before boss 2 arrived. In the 48 s with two bosses up, HP never dropped below ~123/137.
2. **Silhouette flash.** Enemies and bosses turn pure white for 1-2 frames on hit, so the boss always reads as "being hurt" through the particles.
3. **Scale ladder.**

| What | Size |
|---|---|
| Hero | ~1/12 of screen height |
| Fodder | 0.8-1x hero |
| Elites | 1.5-4x hero, plus a crown |
| Timed bosses | 4-6x |
| Gate boss | 6-8x |

   Size alone tells you the threat level.
4. **Text has a colour code.**

| Text | Colour |
|---|---|
| Normal hits | white |
| Crits | yellow tilted "CRIT!" + number |
| Damage taken | red |
| Gold | yellow "$" |
| XP | cyan "+" |
| Stat gains | white name + green value |
| Blocks | orange "Block" |
| Event banners | yellow ("SWARM") or pink-red ("FINAL SWARM"), centre-upper |

5. **World signposts beat the HUD.** A floating "!" over unused shrines, merchants and the portal. A price tag over every chest. A one-line objective.
6. **Menus show the whole build.** Inventory on the left and all ~31 stats on the right, beside every offer, chest and shop. Choices take 1-3 s.

**What gets messy (do not repeat)**
1. **The camera** clips into cliffs, ruins and a body-slamming elite. Foreground roots and props fill 30-100% of the screen for a second at a time.
2. **The player's own red effects** (a red half-dome from the shield weapon) look like enemy telegraphs.
3. **Number spam.** Dozens of overlapping CRIT numbers, some filling 10% of the screen near the camera. Kills stop being readable as single events.
4. **Elite contact damage has no tell.** The 72→7 HP moment read as unfair, not as a mistake.
5. **Off-screen boss.** The gate boss bar dropped for over a minute with the boss never on screen. Only a minimap skull said where it was.
6. **Unexplained floating words** ("Cloak", "Duration") with no visible source.

## 5. Feel moments, ranked by how much they sell the game

1. **The level-up cascade after a swarm warning.**
   - The warning, then a wall of 100+ enemies within 15 s, then 4 level-ups in 20 s (two of them 1 s apart).
   - Each one has a freeze, a ray burst, a rainbow XP bar and usually a free stat popup.
   - Danger and reward peak together. This is the core loop at full volume.
2. **A new weapon pays off on the next frame.** At 1:34 the lightning pick cleared ~20 goblins instantly. Instant proof that the choice mattered.
3. **Chest reveal with hidden rarity.** Beam, slot-machine cycle, starburst, rarity word. The **cyan → pink beam shift** before the Epic is the best single beat in the footage. Even a Common item gets a ceremony.
4. **The screen-clear pickup.** +247 kills and +300 gold in 10 s, with a full-screen fireball. A burst reward that breaks up the grind.
5. **Near death and recovery.** 72→7 HP, the red vignette, kiting at 6 HP while the elite melts, then 25 s of regen. Good tension, spoiled by the missing tell.
6. **Shrine hold under pressure.** Stand still 5 s in a big coloured dome while 80-100 enemies pile up, then a lottery pick of 1 from 3 rarities.
7. **Two boss bars side by side, one dies.** The bar vanishes, the other widens, and a free chest opens moments later: a mid-fight progress beat.
8. **Optional overtime.** The red clock, the climbing ×N badge, and the exit right there. Greed on the player's own terms.
9. **Constant micro-feedback.** $N and +N tallies beside the hero, crit text, green stat popups, a rising chest price as a gold target.
10. **Calm arrival.** Chaos, then black, then a letterboxed arrival, then a palette-swapped biome, a title card, a chest at spawn and **7 s with no enemies**.
