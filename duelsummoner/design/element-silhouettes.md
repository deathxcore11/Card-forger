# Element silhouettes (lab check, 23 Nov 2026)

Each of the six unwired elements is recognisable in a black and white screenshot by shape and motion, not by tint. Lab: `?fxlab`, buttons Shapes and Grey; `node test/browser-shapes.mjs <outdir>` writes elements_colour.png, elements_grey_a.png and elements_grey_b.png (0.45 s apart). The pieces are in `src/view/elshapes.js` (view only, lab-only until the sim wires these elements).

| Element | Silhouette | Motion | What tells it apart in greyscale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ice | a cluster of sharp 4-sided shards of three heights standing on a flat hexagon that lies on the ground | nearly still, a slow sparkle | the only one made of straight spikes on a flat six-sided sheet |
| Poison | a low pooling cloud of flattened round lumps hugging the ground, small bubbles above it | swells and sinks, bubbles rise and pop | the only one that stays under knee height and has no sharp edge |
| Wind | three thin flat ribbons winding up a leaning axis | turn and travel upward | the only one made of curved thin lines, and the only one that leans |
| Earth | heavy tilted slabs of different heights over a ring of cracks | thrust up out of the ground in turn with a hard stop | blocky and angular, wide and chunky, no curves, a crack ring on the ground |
| Light | five tall thin straight columns of different heights on a card-shaped rectangle (5:7) | pulse in height, no sideways motion | the tallest element, strictly vertical, on a rectangle not a circle |
| Dark | a black disc with a thin pale edge, small wisps around it | the wisps move inward and shrink | the only void: black where the others are bright, and the only motion toward its centre |

Wind and light, which looked alike as pale mint against pale cream, no longer do: wind is curved, leaning and turning; light is straight, vertical and still sideways.
