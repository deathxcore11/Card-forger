# Stand-ins (shapes that still wait for real art)

Everything below is a placeholder shape drawn by code. It stays until a model replaces it. Updated 22 Nov 2026.

## Swarm enemies
Every swarm kind is a flat-shaded primitive (green diamond by default; box, icosahedron and octahedron variants). Kinds: Dust Husk, Husk Runner, Glass Seer, Ram Golem, Sand Bloat, Tomb Scarab, Overtime Hunter.

## Pickups
- Heart: pink diamond (stand-in).
- Coin: orange disc (stand-in, spins).
- XP crystal: faceted gem (real shape, glows and bobs).

## Props
- Trees, rocks, bushes, grass and flowers: code-built shapes in the Stage 1 palette (src/view/scenery.js), until the baked models arrive.
- Chest: code-built box with a lid.

## Cards and shots
- Thrown cards are flat quads; fire, lob and bolt shots are octahedra.
