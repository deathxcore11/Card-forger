## Honest note (Act 43, The Hobbit)

The Hobbit pool is small (321 cards) and rare-heavy, and 24-game runs swing by about 15 points on the same decks (battle 4 read 26% to 71% on near-identical lists). In the table, the last column is the final 24-game run (not 10 games this time) and the middle columns are earlier 24-game tuning runs on earlier lists. Pooled across the tuning runs, hero win rates sit near: b1 57%, b2 54%, b3 55%, b4 45%, b5 42%, b6 63%, b7 55%, b8 49%, b9 50%.
Known gaps against the target bands (slots 1-3 60-80, slots 4-7 45-60, boss and secret 30-45): b1-b3 run a few points low, b5 a few low, b6 a few high, b8 and b9 a little high. None is outside simulation noise, but none is proven inside the band either. Equipment (Sting, Orcrist) made the AI heroes weaker in sims, so the final heroes use real creature cores and no equipment.
