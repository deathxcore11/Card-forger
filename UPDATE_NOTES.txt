CARDFORGE 2.2 UPDATE

- Commander bracket estimate (Deck → Engine). Shows the lowest bracket (1–5) the list fits by
  Game Changers, tutors, extra turns and mass land denial, what Cardforge counted, and what to
  cut to drop a bracket. It's a starting point for Rule 0 talks: two-card combos and play style
  aren't detected.
- Mana base helper (Deck → Engine). Suggests a basic-land split from the colored mana symbols in
  your spells and lists lands that always enter tapped.
- Life tracker (Deck → Playtest). 2–6 players at 40 life (20 for 60-card decks), commander damage
  (also comes off life, 21 is lethal), poison (10 is lethal) and a turn counter. "I won / I lost"
  saves the game to the deck's log with the ending turn.
- Proxy print sheet (Deck → Shop, or ⋯ menu for the whole deck). 9 cards per page at real card
  size for playtesting. Basic lands are skipped.
- Publishing: the project now lives on GitHub with automatic tests. Cloudflare can deploy straight
  from GitHub (see README.md); GitHub Pages is available as a second address.

CARDFORGE 2.1 UPDATE — October 12, 2026

- Opening a deck idea now builds a proposed full deck with lands and missing cards. The default purchase budget is your workspace budget (normally $25); you can change it.
- Save entire deck includes every listed card and all basic-land quantities. No automatic ownership changes.
- Custom budgets in suggestions, slot filling and upgrades support values above $100 (up to the app's $100,000 validation limit).
- A dollar budget is separate from Commander's 100 total cards. Lands count toward those 100 cards.
- The planner reserves money for the remaining spells rather than using the budget on an early expensive recommendation. Owned-only mode remains available and can be incomplete.
- Random Card buttons on Home and Search. Random Deck buttons on Home and Decks; previews require review before saving.
- Set badges include readable set codes and rarity-colored symbols in card rows, grids and suggestions. Draft/search badges identify reference printings. Collection entries without a recorded set show SET ? rather than inventing a physical edition.
