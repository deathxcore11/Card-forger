# Lab report, 19 Nov 2026

3,000 expert-brain runs each, seeds 1 to 3000, same seeds for every row. Simulator only; nothing here was judged by a person playing.
Run one row: `CRUCIBLE_CFG='<config>' node tools/crucible/sim.mjs --runs 3000 --seed 1 --level 2 [--family shadow]`. Add `"trackFx":1` for the per-side use counts.

## Gift scarcity (`giftXpScale`: Forge Levels cost this many times more)

| Scale | Gifts a run | Clear | Clear with Tomb Gifts findable | Runs holding each Tomb piece | Same, brain that prefers Tomb |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10.7 | 41.8 | 45.9 | about 80% | about 92%, clear 49.1 |
| 2 | 4.9 | 22.4 | 20.5 | about 31% | about 67%, clear 20.1 |
| 3 | 2.8 | 14.4 | 13.9 | about 16% | about 46%, clear 12.1 |

Gifts are most of the player's power: halving them halves the clear rate. When Gifts are scarce, chasing the Tomb set clears no more often than taking the best Gift on offer.

Long Watch (start of your turn, if you hold a veiled monster, next meld +300 ATK): shadow 27.0 to 30.0. It fires about once a duel, so a veiled monster is still on the table at the start of the player's turn only about once a duel. Veiled walls do not last a rival turn.
