## Overnight 19 to 20 Nov: the first progression loop, offers, free Trap, rival styles

All numbers: 3,000 expert-CPU runs, seeds from 1, five-Gift preset, no Forger unless named. Simulator only. Nothing here was judged by a person.

### What was built

- Feats (`engine/meta.mjs`): Storm Hand (12 melds of four or more cards) unlocks Grounding Rod; Grave Tender (8 duels won with eight or more of your monsters in the graveyard) unlocks Grave Ward; Patient Trap (rivals attack into your veiled monsters or Traps 12 times) unlocks Snare. Progress is banked win or lose. Per run the expert CPU makes 13 four-card melds, wins 6 duels with a full graveyard and is attacked into 8 times (15 on the shadow brain), so it finishes the first feat in run one and the others by run two. A person will be slower; not measured.
- A newly unlocked Gift is shown once, marked NEW, in the first Gift offer of the next run (`newRun` option `featured`).
- Codex: 530 result pages (490 cross melds, 39 two-card printed fusions, 1 three-card printed fusion; counted by trying every pair of the 781 monsters). A found page states the rule that made it and the ATK range that makes it again. An unfound page shows only Type and Attribute.
- Page: run-end screen with feat bars and the unlock, Feats screen, one Gift re-deal a run, "fits your pieces" tags.

### The Heat ladder (`heatRules`, 3,000 expert-CPU runs a level, five-Gift preset, leaning offers, no Forger)

Each level adds one rule the player reads before the run. Rival monsters no longer grow with Heat. Levels stack.

| Heat | Rule | Clear | Past guardian 1 | Past guardian 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | none | 44.7 | 92.8 | 71.2 |
| 1 | Champions' Road: the third chamber of every floor holds only champions and a guardian | 42.5 | 92.2 | 66.0 |
| 2 | Cursed Halls: on floors two and three every ordinary duel is cursed | 42.2 | 92.2 | 64.9 |
| 3 | Provisioned: floor guardians +500 Life Points, first monster +200 ATK | 35.3 | 88.1 | 56.8 |
| 4 | Scarce Rest: Sanctum doors half as often | 33.2 | 87.0 | 55.3 |
| 5 | Seasoned Rivals: every rival fights in its style from the first floor | 31.0 | 86.0 | 53.0 |
| 6 | Dim Halls: one door in each chamber hides its reward | 29.6 | 86.5 | 52.1 |
| 7 | Thin Flame: two Sparks, not three | 23.3 | 78.3 | 42.4 |
| 8 | Hard Law: floor guardians use the hard form of their Law | 22.0 | 78.0 | 41.9 |
| 9 | Crowned Champions: a champion's first monster +400 ATK, +15 Embers for beating one | 14.0 | 70.0 | 30.7 |
| 10 | The Second Gate: the other third-floor guardian waits after the last one | 10.4 | 70.0 | 30.7 |

How it got here. The first draft also made rival monsters 100 ATK stronger at Heat 5 and again at Heat 9, and gave champions two relics at Heat 7. That fell to 20.7 at Heat 5, 6.0 at Heat 7 and 2.6 at Heat 10, with half of all runs dying on the first floor. Both stat bumps were removed (the ladder now adds rules only), the champion bonus was cut to +400, and the levels were reordered so a soft level follows each hard one.

Weak for the CPU, kept on purpose: Cursed Halls (cursed duels pay more, so it is a trade), Dim Halls (the CPU does not read rewards), Hard Law (minus 1.3; a person short a card under the Tyrant will feel it more than the CPU does).

On the final ladder with a Forger: Tarn clears 65.1 at Heat 0, 52.8 at Heat 5 and 23.7 at Heat 10. The Veilwalker clears 10.7 at Heat 10, the same as no Forger.

Climbing profiles (`profile-sim.mjs 100 30 2 1`: rotate Forgers, buy the Vault, always play the highest open Heat): mean Heat open is 5.2 after 10 runs, 8.7 after 20 and 10 after 27. After 30 runs 63 of 100 profiles had cleared Heat 10. The Vault still runs dry at run 9, so from run 10 the next Heat level is the only new thing, and Memories pile up with nothing to buy.
